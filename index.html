Who We Are
Andrés Ortiz · Founder & CEO
ANDRÉS
ORTIZ
Founder · CEO · FOH Engineer
"The sound is invisible.
The result is not."

Colombian sound engineer based in Spain. Over 2,000 shows across Europe, Latin America and the United States. More than 7 years working with world-class Latin urban and pop artists on international tours — from intimate club nights to sold-out arenas and iconic venues like Ushuaïa Ibiza.

OffStage was built on a simple belief: great production should be invisible. The audience never thinks about the sound — they just feel it. That is the standard we hold every night, on every stage, in every city.
